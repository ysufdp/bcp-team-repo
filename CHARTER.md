# Team Charter

## 🎯 Purpose
This document governs our team's collaboration, workflow rules, and repository policies for our software engineering laboratory assignments.

## 👥 Team Roles & Responsibilities
* Roles are currently under review and will be finalized following our initial team discussions and topic exploration.

## 💬 Collaboration & Workflow Rules
1. **GitHub Flow:** All changes flow through feature branches and reviewed pull requests. No direct pushes to `main`.
2. **Review Policy:** Every pull request requires at least one approving review from a teammate before merging.
3. **File Naming:** All deliverables and screenshots must strictly follow the required format (`surname_component.png`).
